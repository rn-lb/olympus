Behind the archive

Stories with many lives

Olympus Archive is a compact introduction to figures from ancient Greek myth. It is designed for discovery: begin with a name, follow a symbol, and use the entry as a path toward deeper reading.

Editorial approach

Greek myths do not form a single fixed canon. Details shift between poets, places, periods, and religious practices. The archive therefore uses careful phrases such as “in one tradition” and notes when parentage varies.

What this project is not

These summaries are not substitutes for primary texts, scholarly translations, or archaeological context. They are orientation points intended to encourage responsible curiosity.

Contribute

Corrections and well-sourced additions are welcome. Read the contribution guide before proposing an entry.
